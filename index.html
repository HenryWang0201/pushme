Development preview · native iOS

Another you, for the next real step.

PushMe is a native iOS app. A companion, another you, helps turn a goal into something you can start today.

Say the goal in everyday language. PushMe drafts a staged plan. You read it, change it, and confirm it. Today then shows the next concrete action, with a time and a duration. You can start it, mark it done, or postpone it by 15 minutes. XP, level, a streak, and five growth dimensions record the progress you keep.

From a goal to the next action

The path is short on purpose. You stay in the loop at every step that matters.

  1. 1 Talk it through

    Describe the goal the way you would to another person. Chat is where you discuss it and adjust what comes next.

  2. 2 Review the plan

    PushMe proposes a staged task plan with AI assistance. You can change it. Nothing is set until you confirm.

  3. 3 Take the next step

    Today shows one concrete action, with its time and duration. Start it, mark it complete, or postpone it by 15 minutes.

  4. 4 See the growth

    Finishing an action adds XP. Level, a streak, and five growth dimensions stay with that progress.

In the app

These are real screens from the iOS app. The interface is in Chinese, and the goals are demo data.

Today keeps the next action close

Sprout, a small green plant, is the current default companion. On Today it sits with the action in front of you. In this demo that action is a 45-minute swim at 19:00, part of learning to swim in two weeks.

The card shows the time, the duration, and the growth tied to finishing it. You can start, mark it done, or postpone by 15 minutes. Level, XP, a streak, and five growth dimensions are on the same screen.

Plan holds the longer view

Plan can show a day, a week, a month, or your goals. This screenshot is the Goals view: three goals, each with a progress ring. They are a two-week swimming goal, 20 minutes of reading, and an evening review.

The shot is not a filled-in calendar. Day, week, and month are in the app. They are simply not what this picture shows.

A companion you can change

The companion is another you. It shows up on Today and in chat. You can switch among colorful official object avatars. Sprout is the current default.

In this screenshot Sprout is selected. Other objects on the grid include a controller, a barbell, a lightbulb, a chat bubble, a watering can, a book, a brain, a bull, a bunny, a camera, and a cat.

Chat, then you decide

Chat is for talking through a goal and changing the next steps. In this demo, wanting to learn freestyle breathing on weekday evenings becomes a 14-day proposal: about 45 minutes a day, after 19:00, starting with getting comfortable in the water.

The plan waits. You can confirm the schedule or ask for a change. Read AI suggestions before you confirm them. You keep control of the plan and of what you actually do.
