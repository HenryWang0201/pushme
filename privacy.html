Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Operator: Yiheng Wang (individual developer) · Contact: support@example.com

This Privacy Policy describes how PushMe (“we”, “us”, or the “App”) handles information when you use our iOS application and this website. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

1. Information we collect

Anonymous device identifier

We generate or use a pseudonymous device identifier to distinguish your task data on our servers and to help diagnose technical issues. It is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Task content

When you enter goals and use AI-assisted breakdown, we process the text you submit and the resulting task plans and reminder copy needed to manage and schedule your tasks.

Usage and progress data

Completion records, streaks, avatar level, and related “five-dimension” stats are stored primarily on your device. Server-side storage is limited to what is needed for synced task management as described below.

Permissions

With your permission, the App uses local notifications for scheduled reminders. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

2. How we use information

  • Provide task breakdown, scheduling, and reminders
  • Store and sync task data associated with your device identifier
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs
  • Respond to support requests and legal obligations

3. AI and third-party processors

Task breakdown and reminder text are generated using large language models called from our servers (not from API keys embedded in the App). By default we may use DeepSeek or another OpenAI-compatible provider; the provider may change as we maintain the service.

Content sent to the model is limited to what is needed to generate task plans and reminder wording. Outputs in the App are labeled as AI-assisted where applicable. We do not use your content to train public models on your behalf unless a future feature clearly asks for your consent.

4. Storage and retention

Task data tied to your device identifier is stored on our servers and isolated per device. Avatar progression and streak data remain on your device unless a future account feature changes how data is synced (we will update this policy before that happens).

We retain data only as long as needed to provide the service or as required by law. You may delete local data by removing the App, and request deletion of server-side data as described in Section 6.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not show third-party ads or use tracking for advertising.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the age required for consent in their region (see Section 8).

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Residents of the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and California (and similar jurisdictions) may contact us to exercise these rights.

Deletion: Uninstalling the App removes on-device data. To request deletion of server-side data associated with your device, email support@example.com from the address you used for support (if any) and describe your request. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify the request.

We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. International transfers

We and our AI infrastructure providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

8. Children

PushMe is not directed at children under 13 in the United States, or under 16 in the European Economic Area, without verifiable parental or guardian consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information without consent, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted in the App and/or on this site with an updated effective date.
