Support & FAQ

Operator: Yiheng Wang (individual developer) · support@example.com

Reminders are not firing

PushMe uses iOS local notifications for scheduled nudges. Check that notifications are enabled for PushMe in Settings → Notifications → PushMe, and that Focus modes or Scheduled Summary are not blocking alerts. Reminders only work if the App has permission and your device clock is correct.

How do I delete my data?

On your device: Deleting PushMe removes locally stored streaks, avatar stats, and other on-device data.

On our servers: Task data linked to your device identifier can be deleted on request. Email support@example.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and any details that help us locate your data. See the Privacy Policy for your regional rights (access, deletion, etc.).

AI-generated content

Task plans and reminder text may be produced with AI assistance and are marked accordingly in the App. AI output can be wrong or inappropriate for your situation. Always review suggestions before confirming tasks. PushMe is not a substitute for professional advice.

Report inappropriate AI content

If the App surfaces AI text that is offensive, harmful, or otherwise inappropriate, email support@example.com with a screenshot or description, the language used, and what you were trying to do. We use reports to improve prompts and safety handling.

Availability and regions

PushMe is published on the App Store in all regions except mainland China. For billing, refunds, and download issues, Apple’s support processes apply in addition to contacting us.

Contact

For privacy requests, bugs, or general help:

support@example.com

We aim to reply within a reasonable time. Include your iOS version and App version when reporting technical issues.
